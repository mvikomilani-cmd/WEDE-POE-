Classic Margherita Pizza

Pizza · Prep: 15 mins · Cook: 8 to 12 mins · Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • 300 g pizza dough
  • 100 g mozzarella
  • Tomato sauce
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Olive oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to its highest setting.
  2. Stretch the dough to a 25 cm circle.
  3. Spread the tomato sauce and add the mozzarella.
  4. Bake for 5 to 8 minutes until the crust is charred.
  5. Finish with basil and a drizzle of olive oil.

GudEats Signature Beef Burger

Burgers · Prep: 10 mins · Cook: 8 to 10 mins · Serves: 1

Ingredients

  • 200 g beef mince
  • 1 burger bun
  • 1 slice cheddar
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Pickles
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Shape the mince into a patty and season.
  2. Grill the patty to your preferred doneness.
  3. Toast the bun and assemble the burger with the toppings and sauce.

Pad Thai (Prawn)

Noodles · Prep: 15 mins · Cook: 10 mins · Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Rice noodles
  • Prawns
  • Tamarind paste
  • Peanuts
  • Bean sprouts
  • Eggs

Method

  1. Soak the noodles in warm water.
  2. Stir-fry the prawns, then add the sauce.
  3. Toss in the noodles and finish with peanuts and bean sprouts.

Chocolate Lava Cake

Dessert · Prep: 15 mins · Cook: 10 to 12 mins · Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Dark chocolate
  • Butter
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Flour
  • Vanilla
  • Ice cream (optional)

Method

  1. Melt the chocolate and butter together.
  2. Whisk in the eggs and sugar, then fold in the flour.
  3. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until the edges are set.

Grilled Salmon with Lemon Butter
